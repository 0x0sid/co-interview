Neverblank is a real-time interview assistant. While Live is on, it transcribes the conversation on the
device and, when the user taps Generate, suggests an answer they can read.

**How to review the paid features**

- Every new install includes **2 free AI answers** in total (not a subscription trial, nothing is
  charged, they do not renew).
- Tap **Start interview**, accept the one-time AI-processing consent, and allow the microphone and speech
  recognition. Ask a question aloud (for example "How would you design a rate limiter?") and tap the
  **Generate** button (the sparkle at the bottom). Repeat once.
- After the second answer, "Unlock Pro" appears inline. The **third** Generate opens the subscription
  screen. Use the sandbox account below to subscribe to **Neverblank Pro Monthly**; answers then continue
  in the same interview, including the one that was waiting.
- Restore Purchases, Terms of Use, Privacy Policy and Support are on the subscription screen and under
  Settings › Subscription on the home screen.

**Sandbox account:** [email] / [password]

**Data and AI processing**

- Audio is not recorded or uploaded; speech recognition runs on the device.
- When the user taps Generate, the transcript text, their note and excerpts of files they attached are
  sent to Neverblank's backend, which uses a third-party AI model provider to write the answer. The app
  asks for consent before the first Live interview (Guideline 5.1.2(i)).
- No advertising, no tracking.
