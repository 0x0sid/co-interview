# neverblank.io — website handoff

What the site must say so it matches the app. The site returned 404 on every path on 2026-09-26; the
three pages below are required before submission and their URLs go into the app's build settings
(`NEVERBLANK_TERMS_URL`, `NEVERBLANK_PRIVACY_URL`, `NEVERBLANK_SUPPORT_URL`) and App Store Connect.

## Required pages

| Page | Suggested URL | Must include |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Terms of Use | https://neverblank.io/terms | Auto-renewing subscription terms (Apple's standard EULA may be referenced), cancellation via Apple, the free-answer allowance |
| Privacy Policy | https://neverblank.io/privacy | What leaves the phone (transcript text, notes, file excerpts — on Generate), third-party AI processing, RevenueCat (purchase and app user id), installation identifier and access metadata, no audio upload, no tracking, retention, contact |
| Support | https://neverblank.io/support | Contact email, how to restore purchases, how to cancel |

## Pricing and free-use wording

- "**2 free AI answers** with every new install. No subscription needed to try it."
- Paid plan: **Neverblank Pro Monthly** — price shown as Apple's localized price in the app. Do not
  publish a fixed price on the site until the App Store price is confirmed.
- Do **not** say "free trial", "30 seconds", "5 answers" or "free preview": those described earlier
  designs and are no longer true.
